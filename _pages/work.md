## Selected publications

### Dirichlet Diffusion Score Model for Biological Sequence Generation

Introduces a diffusion model for discrete biological sequences, with applications to constrained sequence generation and human promoter design. Published at ICML (2023). [Paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/avdeyev23a.html)

### A Complete Reference Genome Improves Analysis of Human Genetic Variation

Collaborative work showing how the T2T-CHM13 human reference improves mapping and variant analysis. Published in *Science* (2022). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abl3533)

### Comparative Genomics Meets Topology: a Novel View on Genome Median and Halving Problems

Uses topological ideas to formulate and study genome median and halving problems. Published in *BMC Bioinformatics* (2016). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12859-016-1263-7)
